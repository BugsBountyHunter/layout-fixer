# Code of Conduct

Layout Fixer is built for people who write in more than one language. Everyone is welcome here,
whatever language they write in, their background or their level of experience.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexual content or personal attacks.
- Don't publish anyone's private information, including private messages pasted into bug reports.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run by the project, and
when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@BugsBountyHunter](https://github.com/BugsBountyHunter),
through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/BugsBountyHunter/layout-fixer/security/advisories/new)
if you need privacy. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
